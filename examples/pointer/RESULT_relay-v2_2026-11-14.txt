WHAT IT SHOWS (design faults, all three in the judgment channel)
 1. Writers and condensers put facts into JUDGE lines instead of FACT lines. JUDGE allows no numbers, so the figures fell out; the
    judgment channel became the rewrite channel. Q2-Q4 v2 briefs carried no fact sentences at all. Fix: the brief must carry its key
    facts as code-rendered FACT lines (enforced: each JUDGE line's cited facts render with it).
 2. Every v2 error sits in a JUDGE line. The worst is an absence claim: hop 2 dropped the Blackwell compute cells as "background", the
    ledger recorded it, no later hop saw it, and hop 4 reasoned "no figure in the record". Fix: any absence claim ("not stated", "no
    figure", "unmeasured") is checked by code against the full store and the drop list; the drop list travels with the chain.
 3. Most silent drops came from code deleting a rejected JUDGE line along with the only citation of its facts, plus IDs both dropped
    and cited at one hop. Fix: a rejected line goes back to the agent once; never deleted; drop-and-cite at one hop is a format error.
 Plain arm at 5 hops on Haiku made fewer errors than the 3-hop runs A-C; this chain style (structured notes, no figures demanded)
 gives the plain arm little room to err, so the setting must change before another relay can count.
