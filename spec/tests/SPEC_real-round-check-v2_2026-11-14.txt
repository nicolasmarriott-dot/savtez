SAVTEZ REAL-ROUND CHECK v2 SPEC (2026-11-14 night; written before the round was sent)
Purpose: test the new split on a real outside-AI research round. The researcher's quoted sentence and figure are the FACT, checked by
 code against full page text. Its own one-line summary is the JUDGMENT, the only thing sent to the model checker. Researcher: Grok
 (training data stays with Grok), fresh chat, one file in, one reply out.
QUESTION: what has published work measured about one LLM checking another LLM's output: how often the checker misses real errors,
 how often it flags correct output, how far checkers share the generator's blind spots, and which checker designs cut false alarms
 without losing catches.
INPUT: Grok's 15 pipe lines: N | figure | title | canonical URL | exact sentence | location | JUDGMENT: one sentence. Saved verbatim
 as grok_round.txt with sha256.
STORE: Checker A fetches every URL once (curl plus pdftotext; abstract plus every paragraph holding a cited sentence or figure) into one
 file. A 403, 410, empty extraction or missing paragraph = ACCESS FAIL, never "text absent".
FACT CHECKS (code only): F1 title matches the fetched page; F2 the exact sentence appears in the fetched text after normalizing
 spaces, quotes, dashes, hyphenation breaks and TeX escapes (35\% = 35%); F3 the figure appears as a number in that sentence
 (rounding to stated precision, separators, K/M/B, x/times allowed).
JUDGMENT CHECKS: J1 a Haiku checker sees the question, the quoted sentence and the paragraph holding it, and the judgment; it answers
 only "supported: yes/no" plus one reason line. No "keeps qualifiers" question. J2 code scope test, both directions, fixed lists:
 DROP = a word from {only, some, up to, at most, at least, approximately, about, nearly, may, might, can, could} present in the
 quoted sentence and absent from the judgment. WIDEN = a word from {all, always, never, none, every, most, majority} present in
 the judgment and absent from the quoted sentence. Word-boundary match, case-insensitive.
PIPELINE FLAG on a line = any of F1, F2, F3, J1 = no, DROP, WIDEN. ACCESS FAIL lines are reported and left out of both bars.
GROUND TRUTH: a separate Opus agent fetches every URL itself and judges two parts separately, before any pipeline result exists:
 FACT (source real and matching, sentence verbatim as far as its fetch shows, figure right) and JUDGMENT (faithful: no widening,
 no wrong relation, no invented condition, no conflation, no savings or limits misread). Error line = either part wrong. Verdicts
 written to a sealed file first.
BARS (fixed now): catch >= 0.8 of ground-truth error lines; false alarms <= 10% of ground-truth clean lines (max 1 if fewer than 10
 clean). The checker bars count only if ground truth finds >= 4 error lines (the baseline must have room to err).
HEADLINE (reported whatever the bars): wrong facts carried = lines where code passed F1-F3 but ground truth says the FACT is wrong.
 Target 0. Also reported per component (code, J1, DROP, WIDEN) for diagnosis, not scored.
PREDICTION (recorded before the run): DROP is the likeliest false-alarm source (a one-line judgment drops "may" or "up to" that
 doesn't change its truth); WIDEN fires rarely and mostly on real errors; Grok's quotes are less exact than DeepSeek's because Grok
 paraphrases more readily, so F2 fails will be higher than last round's 0 of 12.
LOGGING: every Grok error goes to the result file (rule 10), split into fact errors, judgment errors and bad URLs.
