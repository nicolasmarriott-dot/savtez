== GRAMMAR ==
- Part = exactly 3 letters, consonant-vowel-consonant, lowercase.
- Any two parts differ in at least 2 positions (checked: 23 parts, 0 failures).
- Word = 1-4 parts, head last, modifiers left to right narrowing the head. No separators.
- dax negates only the part immediately after it.
- No synonyms: a compound equal in meaning to an existing word is rejected.
- Unknown part or word = reject the message, never guess.
- Messages open with a version tag: §L0.2
- Critical messages (bix items, money figures, verdicts, report IDs) end with # + first 8 hex of sha256 of the normalized body, computed by tool. If there is no hashing tool, echo back every figure and ID instead.
- Admission: used often | one meaning | saves >=3 tokens vs its phrase on every target tokenizer (cl100k, o200k, DeepSeek, Claude) | passes a cold test with no confident wrong guesses.
